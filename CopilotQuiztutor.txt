Integrating Email and Draft Saving in Copilot Studio

1. Email Integration

Use Power Platform connectors in Copilot Studio to connect your agent to email services like Outlook or Gmail.
Steps:
Go to your agent in Copilot Studio.
Navigate to the Tools page and select “Add a tool.”
Choose “Connector” and search for your email service (e.g., Outlook).
Select the desired email actions (such as sending or reading emails) and add them to your agent.
If a connection doesn’t exist, create a new one by signing in and providing necessary authentication details​1​.
2. Draft Saving

To enable draft saving, you can use connectors to link your agent to data storage services (such as Microsoft Dataverse or SharePoint).
Steps:
In your agent’s Tools or Knowledge section, add a connector for your preferred storage service.
Configure actions to save, retrieve, or update draft content.
You can manage these connections in the Connection Settings page, where you can view, create, and edit connections as needed​2​.
3. Extending Agent Capabilities

You can extend your agent’s functionality by adding custom connectors for services not covered by prebuilt options.
Make sure you have the necessary credentials and permissions to connect to external systems.
After adding tools and knowledge sources, publish your agent to make these features available​3​.
Summary of Steps:

Add email and storage connectors via the Tools page.
Configure actions for sending emails and saving drafts.
Manage connections and authentication in Connection Settings.
